Method notes & corrections
§4.25 math: ratings sort high→low; each subsequent rating applies to the remaining "whole person" (c = a + b×(100−a)/100); final result rounds to the nearest 10. Bilateral factor (§4.26) doesn't apply to this set — no paired-extremity pair is rated.

Anti-pyramiding (§4.14): MI residuals (DC 7006 after the window) and hypertensive heart disease/LVH (DC 7007) rate under the SAME General Rating Formula — one heart rating, take the highest tier that fits. Hypertension (DC 7101) IS separately ratable alongside heart disease (7101 Note 3). The heart card below models this correctly.

Correction vs the repo estimate: rating-estimate.md had cervical flexion 31–40° at 20%. Actual 2025 text: flexion >30–40° (or spasm/tenderness without abnormal gait) = 10%; flexion >15–30°, combined ROM ≤170°, or spasm severe enough to distort gait/contour = 20%.

METs caveat: §4.100(b) still requires METs testing before finalizing 10/30% heart tiers unless contraindicated or 100% applies elsewhere — the 30% echo-hypertrophy floor survives that testing requirement.
